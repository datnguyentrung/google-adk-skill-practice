# 2. Product Overview

## 2.1. Introduction

**An Tam Online Term Deposit** is a VND-denominated term deposit product for individual customers.

Customers can open, monitor, and perform activities related to their term deposits directly through the digital banking application without visiting a branch.

The product is designed for customers who:

- Have idle funds for a defined period of time.
- Want to earn a better return than keeping all funds in a payment account.
- Regularly use digital banking services.
- Prefer a fast and convenient deposit-opening process.
- Do not need immediate access to the entire amount they intend to deposit.

## 2.2. Product Purpose

An Tam Online Term Deposit helps customers proactively allocate funds that are not expected to be used in the short or medium term into a term deposit.

The product is suitable for the following primary needs:

- Safe financial accumulation.
- Optimization of idle funds.
- Financial preparation for a future goal.
- Management of multiple savings deposits with different tenors.
- Completing the entire deposit-opening process through a digital channel.

## 3.2. Tenors

An Tam Online Term Deposit supports the following tenors:

- 1 month.
- 3 months.
- 6 months.
- 9 months.
- 12 months.

When opening a deposit, customers should select a tenor that matches the period during which they expect not to use the funds.

Employees should not provide advice based solely on the highest interest rate. They should first determine when the customer may need to use the funds again.

---

## 3.3. Interest Rates

The basic interest rate schedule effective from **01/07/2026** is as follows:

| Tenor | Basic Interest Rate |
|---|---:|
| 1 month | 3.20% per annum |
| 3 months | 3.80% per annum |
| 6 months | 5.00% per annum |
| 9 months | 5.20% per annum |
| 12 months | 5.50% per annum |

The interest rate is determined when the customer opens the deposit and remains unchanged throughout the deposit tenor, except where the customer withdraws the deposit before maturity.

Interest is calculated based on:

- Principal balance.
- Applicable interest rate.
- Actual number of days the deposit is maintained.
- Interest calculation basis of 365 days per year.

The interest rates above do not include additional interest offered under active promotional programs.

## 3.4. Interest Payment Method

An Tam Online Term Deposit applies the following interest payment method:

**Interest paid at maturity.**

On the maturity date, the bank processes the principal and interest according to the maturity instruction selected by the customer.

Customers can view the following information:

- Principal amount.
- Applicable interest rate.
- Opening date.
- Maturity date.
- Estimated interest amount.

through the digital banking application.

---

## 3.5. Early Withdrawal

Customers are allowed to fully withdraw their An Tam Online Term Deposit before the maturity date.

The product currently **does not support partial withdrawal of principal before maturity**.

When a customer withdraws the deposit before maturity:

- The entire deposit is closed.
- The original term-deposit interest rate no longer applies.
- The entire actual deposit period is subject to the non-term interest rate of **0.20% per annum**.
- The principal and actual interest received are transferred back to the customer's payment account.

Employees must clearly explain this policy before customers select a long-term tenor.

### Advisory Recommendation

If a customer indicates that they may need to use part of the funds during the deposit period, employees may recommend splitting the total amount into several smaller deposits instead of placing all funds into one deposit.

For example, instead of depositing 300 million VND into a single deposit, the customer may open three separate deposits depending on their expected liquidity needs.

---

## 3.6. Maturity Processing

Customers can choose one of the following three maturity options:

### Option 1 – Pay Out Both Principal and Interest

The entire principal and interest amount is transferred to the customer's payment account at maturity.

### Option 2 – Renew Principal Only

The principal is automatically rolled over into a new deposit with a tenor equivalent to the previous tenor.

Interest is transferred to the customer's payment account.

The interest rate for the new tenor is determined according to the applicable interest rate schedule on the renewal date.

### Option 3 – Renew Both Principal and Interest

The entire principal and interest amount is used to create a new deposit for the next tenor.

The new interest rate is determined according to the policy applicable on the renewal date.

Customers may change their maturity instruction through the application before the maturity date.

---

## 3.7. Fees

The bank does not charge fees for:

- Opening an An Tam Online Term Deposit.
- Maintaining the deposit.
- Withdrawing at maturity.
- Withdrawing before maturity through the application.

Early withdrawal does not incur a transaction fee, but the customer is financially affected because the deposit interest is recalculated using the non-term interest rate.

# 4. Customer Segments

These customers receive regular income and often have unused balances after each salary payment cycle.

Common characteristics include:

- Relatively stable incoming cash flows.
- Ability to allocate part of their income to savings.

These customers prefer online experiences and do not want to visit branches for simple transactions.

- Frequent use of the digital banking application.
- Ability to perform transactions independently.

## 4.3. Customers with Idle Funds

These customers often maintain relatively large balances in their payment accounts without having specific plans to use the funds for a certain period.

Examples include:

- Customers who have recently received a bonus.
- Customers with accumulated savings that are not currently needed.
- Customers who have recently received a large amount of income.
- Customers saving for plans expected to occur within the next 6–12 months.

Employees should clarify when the customer expects to use the funds before recommending a tenor.

# 6. Eligibility Conditions

## 6.1. Mandatory Conditions

Customers who want to open an An Tam Online Term Deposit must satisfy all of the following conditions:

1. Be an individual customer.
2. Have an active VND payment account with the bank.
3. Have completed identity verification in accordance with the bank's regulations.
4. Have registered for and activated digital banking services.
5. Be able to log in and confirm transactions through the application.
6. Have an available balance at least equal to the intended deposit amount.
7. Deposit at least **1,000,000 VND** per deposit.
8. Ensure that the source account is not blocked or restricted in a manner that prevents debits.

---

## 6.2. Situations Where the Product Cannot Yet Be Opened

A customer cannot open a deposit if any of the following conditions apply:

- Identity verification has not been completed.
- Digital banking services are locked.
- The payment account is inactive.
- The source account has insufficient available balance.
- The account is in a status that does not allow debits.
- The deposit amount is below the minimum amount.
- The transaction exceeds the permitted digital-channel limit.
- The product has been temporarily suspended according to a bank announcement.

Employees must distinguish between a customer who is **not suitable for the product** and a customer who **does not currently satisfy the transaction conditions**.

# 9. Advisory Process

## Step 1 – Discover Customer Needs

Employees should understand the customer's situation before introducing the product.

Suggested questions include:

- How long do you expect not to need this money?
- Do you have any major spending plans within the next 3–12 months?
- Do you prioritize higher returns or liquidity?
- Is this your entire emergency fund?
- Do you frequently use the banking application?
- Would you prefer to place the funds into one deposit or split them into several deposits?

---

## Step 2 – Assess Product Suitability

Employees should evaluate the customer based on:

- The amount the customer can allocate to savings.
- The period during which the customer expects not to use the funds.
- Liquidity requirements.
- Ability to use digital banking.
- Product eligibility conditions.
- Probability that the customer may need to withdraw before maturity.

If the customer may need the entire amount in the very short term, employees should not recommend a long tenor simply because it offers a higher interest rate.

---

## Step 3 – Recommend a Tenor

The following guidance may be used for reference:

| Customer Need | Suggested Direction |
|---|---|
| May need the money after approximately 1 month | 1-month tenor |
| Does not expect to use the money for approximately 3 months | 3-month tenor |
| Plans to use the money after approximately 6 months | 6-month tenor |
| Funds can remain unused for 6–9 months | Consider a 6- or 9-month tenor |
| Certain that the funds will not be needed for approximately 1 year | Consider a 12-month tenor |
| Timing of future fund usage is uncertain | Consider splitting the funds into multiple deposits |

This is advisory guidance and is not a mandatory rule.

# 10. Sample Advisory Script

### Scenario

The customer has approximately **300 million VND in idle funds for 6–9 months**, wants to earn additional returns, and does not want to visit a branch.

---

**Employee:** Hello, Mr. Minh. I understand that you currently have some funds that you do not expect to use immediately. May I ask how long you expect not to need this money?

**Customer:** Probably around 6 to 9 months. I currently have almost 300 million VND sitting in my account and I do not need it right now.

**Employee:** During that period, is there a possibility that you may need to use the entire 300 million VND for work or another major expense?

**Customer:** Probably not, but I would still like to keep some money available in case of an emergency.

**Employee:** In that case, you do not necessarily need to deposit the entire 300 million VND. You can keep part of it in your payment account for emergencies, while the amount you are confident you will not need can be placed in An Tam Online Term Deposit.

**Customer:** Do I have to go to the bank to open this product?

**Employee:** No. You can open it directly through the application. The funds will be transferred from your payment account into the term deposit, and you can monitor the deposit directly through the application.

**Customer:** What is the interest rate if I choose a 6-month tenor?

**Employee:** The current basic interest rate for a 6-month tenor is 5.00% per annum. The bank is currently running the Online Savings Plus 2026 program. If you open a deposit of at least 100 million VND through the application and choose a tenor of at least 6 months, the deposit receives an additional 0.20% per annum, provided all program conditions are satisfied. Therefore, the 6-month tenor would have an applicable rate of 5.20% per annum if your transaction meets all program requirements.

**Customer:** What about a 9-month deposit?

**Employee:** The basic interest rate for a 9-month tenor is 5.20% per annum, and if the promotional conditions are satisfied, the applicable rate would be 5.40% per annum. However, I recommend choosing the tenor based on when you expect to need the funds rather than selecting solely based on the interest rate.

**Customer:** What happens if I need the money while the deposit is still active?

**Employee:** You may withdraw the deposit before maturity, but the product currently does not support partial withdrawals. If you withdraw early, the entire deposit will be closed, and the actual deposit period will only earn the non-term interest rate of 0.20% per annum.

**Customer:** So what should I do if I am worried that I might need part of the money?

**Employee:** You can split the funds into several deposits. For example, you can place the amount you are confident you will not need for six months into a separate deposit and keep another portion in your payment account or open another smaller term deposit. If you later need money, you will not have to close the entire amount you originally deposited.

**Customer:** I think depositing 200 million VND for 6 months and keeping 100 million VND available would make sense.

**Employee:** With a 200 million VND deposit and a 6-month tenor, if you open it during the promotional period through the application, the deposit meets the minimum 100 million VND threshold for consideration for the additional 0.20% per annum interest benefit.

**Customer:** I can open it directly on my phone, right?

**Employee:** Yes. Log in to the application, go to Savings & Investments, select An Tam Online Term Deposit, enter 200 million VND, select a 6-month tenor, and review the displayed interest rate before confirming. I can guide you through each step if needed.

# 11. Handling Common Objections

## 11.1. “What happens if I need to withdraw the money before maturity?”

Customers may fully withdraw the term deposit before the maturity date.

However, if the deposit is withdrawn before maturity, the original term-deposit interest rate no longer applies. The entire actual deposit period will earn the non-term interest rate of 0.20% per annum.

If the customer expects that they may need part of the funds, employees should recommend splitting the funds into multiple deposits from the beginning.

---

## 11.2. “Is online saving safe?”

An Tam Online Term Deposit is opened and managed within the bank's digital banking system.

Customers must log in and complete the authentication method required by the application before completing the transaction.

Employees should remind customers:

- Never disclose their login password.
- Never disclose authentication codes to another person.
- Never perform transactions through unknown or suspicious links.
- Only use the bank's official application.

---

## 11.3. “Why don't I just keep the money in my payment account?”

A payment account is suitable for funds that customers use frequently and for which high liquidity is required.

A term deposit is more appropriate for funds that customers determine they will not need for a certain period of time.

Therefore, customers do not necessarily have to choose one or the other. They may retain funds needed for spending in the payment account while transferring idle funds into An Tam Online Term Deposit.

---

## 11.4. “Can I open multiple term deposits?”

Yes.

Customers may open multiple separate An Tam Online Term Deposits, provided that each deposit has a minimum amount of 1,000,000 VND.

Splitting funds into multiple deposits may be appropriate for customers who:

- Have multiple financial goals.
- Want to use different tenors.
- Want to reduce the likelihood of having to withdraw the entire deposited amount when an unexpected liquidity need arises.

# 14. Notes for Sales Advisors

## 14.1. Do Not Provide Advice Based Solely on Interest Rates

The interest rate is an important factor, but it is not the only factor.

Employees should consider:

- When the customer will need the funds.
- The amount required for emergencies.
- Liquidity needs.
- The likelihood of early withdrawal.
- The customer's financial objectives.

---

## 14.2. Clearly Explain the Early Withdrawal Policy

This information must be explained whenever the customer appears uncertain about how long they can keep the funds deposited.

Customers must understand that early withdrawal may significantly reduce the actual interest received compared with the amount originally expected.

---

## 14.3. Do Not Assume Customers Automatically Qualify for Promotions

Employees should only communicate a promotional interest rate after checking:

- Program period.
- Deposit amount.
- Tenor.
- Product-opening channel.
- Other promotional programs currently being applied.

The interest rate displayed for the transaction in the application at the time the customer confirms the transaction must be checked as the final reference.

---

## 14.4. Distinguish Product Eligibility from Promotional Eligibility

Customers only need at least **1,000,000 VND** to open an An Tam Online Term Deposit, provided they satisfy the general product conditions.

The **100,000,000 VND** threshold applies only when determining eligibility for the Online Savings Plus 2026 promotional benefit.

Employees must not refuse to provide product advice to customers depositing less than 100 million VND on the grounds that they do not qualify for the promotional program.

---

## 14.5. Prioritize Customer Suitability

The objective of advisory is not to transfer the maximum possible amount from a customer's payment account into a term deposit.

Employees should help customers determine a suitable allocation between:

**Funds for short-term needs + emergency funds + funds available for saving.**

Only the portion of funds that customers genuinely do not expect to use during an appropriate period should be considered for An Tam Online Term Deposit.
