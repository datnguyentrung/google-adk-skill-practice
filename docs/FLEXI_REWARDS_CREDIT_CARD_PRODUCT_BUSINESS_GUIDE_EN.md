# 2. Product Overview

## 2.1. What is the Flexi Rewards Credit Card?

**Flexi Rewards** is a credit card for individual customers that allows customers to use a credit limit granted by the bank to pay for goods and services in advance and then repay the bank according to the statement cycle.

The card can be used for:

- In-person payments at card-accepting merchants.
- Online payments.
- Domestic and international payments.
- Purchases of goods.
- Payments for services.
- Dining expenses.
- Booking airline tickets, hotels, and travel services.
- Registration for certain recurring payment services.
- Eligible installment transactions.
- Cash withdrawals within the permitted limit.

The product is suitable for customers with stable income, the ability to manage spending, and a need for a payment method with flexible repayment timing.

---

## 2.2. Nature of the Credit Limit

Unlike a debit card, customers do not necessarily need to have the full amount available in their payment account before making a transaction with a credit card.

The bank grants the customer a **credit limit**.

The customer may use this limit to make payments and then repay the amount used to the bank.

Example:

A customer is granted a credit limit of **100 million VND**.

If the customer makes a purchase transaction worth 20 million VND and the transaction has been posted, the remaining available credit limit is, in principle, approximately **80 million VND**, excluding pending transactions, holds, or other obligations.

When the customer pays 20 million VND into the card account and the payment is posted, the available credit limit may be restored accordingly.

---

## 2.3. Basic Difference Between a Credit Card and a Debit Card

### Debit Card

The customer uses money already available in their payment account.

### Credit Card

The customer uses a credit limit granted by the bank and is obligated to repay it in accordance with applicable rules.

Employees must explain that money used through a credit card **is not free money** and is not a permanent addition to the customer's income.

# 4. Credit Limit

## 4.1. Principles for Determining the Credit Limit

The credit limit is determined by the bank based on a comprehensive credit assessment.

Factors that may be considered include:

- Monthly income.
- Employment stability.
- Length of employment.
- Credit history.
- Current outstanding debt.
- Limits and outstanding balances on other credit cards.
- Monthly debt obligations.
- Existing relationship with the bank.
- Ability to provide evidence of income.
- Credit risk assessment results.

Customers may request a desired credit limit, but the bank is not obligated to grant the exact amount requested.

---

## 4.2. Example of Available Credit Limit

The customer has a credit limit of:

**100,000,000 VND**

During the cycle, the customer has made:

- Purchases: 15 million VND.
- Airline ticket payment: 8 million VND.
- Online services: 2 million VND.

The total posted transactions amount to 25 million VND.

If there are no holds or other obligations, the remaining available credit limit is approximately:

**75 million VND.**

After the customer repays 20 million VND and the payment is posted, the available credit limit may increase accordingly.

---

## 4.3. Credit Limit Adjustment

The bank may consider increasing or decreasing the credit limit during the period of card use.

A customer requesting a credit limit increase may be required to provide:

- Updated income documents.
- Employment information.
- Account statements.
- Documents proving changes in financial capacity.

The bank may also proactively review the credit limit if it identifies:

- Changes in the customer's financial capacity.
- Signs of increased credit risk.
- Poor payment history.
- Unusual information related to the card account.

# 5. Card Application Eligibility

## 5.1. Conditions for Accepting an Application

Individual customers may submit an application when they meet the basic conditions:

- At least 20 years old at the time of application.
- Not older than 60 years at the time of application under normal policy.
- Have valid identification documents.
- Have verifiable residence information.
- Have lawful employment or a lawful source of income.
- Be able to provide proof of income.
- Normally have net income of at least **10 million VND/month**.
- Have an appropriate credit history.
- Not fall under cases restricted from receiving credit under applicable regulations.

For salaried customers, working at the current employer for **at least 03 months** and having a total continuous employment period of at least 12 months may be viewed more favorably.

---

## 5.2. Meeting Initial Conditions Does Not Mean Approval

The fact that a customer:

- Earns more than 10 million VND.
- Meets the age requirement.
- Has an employment contract.
- Has a salary account.

does not mean the customer will definitely be issued a card.

The bank also assesses:

- Existing financial obligations.
- Credit quality.
- Repayment ability.
- Stability of income sources.
- Actual application documents.
- Other risk factors.

Employees must not commit in advance to a credit limit or approval result.

---

## 5.3. Cases Requiring Additional Appraisal

Some cases may require deeper review:

- Customers with multiple loans.
- Customers with multiple credit cards at other institutions.
- Existing card limits that are high relative to income.
- Customers using a large portion of their existing credit limit.
- History of late payments.
- Income from multiple sources.
- Highly variable income.
- Self-employed customers.
- Employment or residence information that is difficult to verify.

# 9. Interest-Free Period

## 9.1. Principle

Flexi Rewards provides a maximum interest-free period of approximately:

**50 days**

for eligible purchase transactions.

The actual period depends on the transaction date.

A transaction made near the beginning of the cycle may receive a longer interest-free period than one made close to the statement date.

---

## 9.2. Example

The statement date is the **20th of each month**.

The due date is the **10th of the following month**.

The customer makes a purchase on 21/08.

This transaction may appear on the statement dated 20/09, and the customer may have until 10/10 to make payment.

If the interest-free conditions are met, the transaction may receive nearly the maximum interest-free period.

By contrast, a transaction made on 18/09 that also appears on the 20/09 statement will have a significantly shorter period between the transaction date and payment due date.

---

## 9.3. Conditions for Maintaining the Interest-Free Benefit

The customer must pay the **full statement balance** in accordance with the required conditions and by the due date.

If the customer only makes the minimum payment or does not fully pay as required by policy, the remaining balance may incur interest.

---

## 9.4. Transactions That Do Not Receive the Standard Purchase Interest-Free Period

Cash withdrawals made with a credit card do not receive the same interest-free period as purchase transactions.

Interest on cash withdrawals is calculated according to policy from the time the transaction is posted.

Employees should not present cash withdrawal as a primary benefit of the card.

---

# 10. Outstanding Balance Payment

## 10.1. Full Payment of the Statement Balance

This should be the preferred option explained to customers who have suitable financial capacity.

If the customer pays the full amount on time, eligible purchase transactions may continue to qualify for the interest-free policy.

---

## 10.2. Minimum Payment

The standard minimum payment is:

**5% of the total statement balance**, but not less than **200,000 VND**.

If the total outstanding balance is less than 200,000 VND, the customer must pay the full balance.

Making the minimum payment helps the customer satisfy the minimum payment obligation for the cycle.

However:

- The unpaid balance remains outstanding.
- The outstanding balance may continue to incur interest.
- Eligibility for interest-free treatment of transactions may be affected.

Employees must not advise that paying only the minimum amount each month is an optimal way to use the card.

---

## 10.3. Failure to Pay on Time

A customer who fails to meet the minimum payment obligation on time may:

- Incur a late payment fee.
- Incur interest.
- Have their credit history affected.
- Have card usage restricted or temporarily suspended.
- Be subject to a credit limit review or adjustment.

---

# 11. Cash Withdrawal

Flexi Rewards allows customers to withdraw cash within the granted limit.

The standard maximum cash withdrawal limit is:

**50% of the credit limit.**

For example, if a customer is granted a 100 million VND credit limit, the maximum cash withdrawal limit will, in principle, not exceed 50 million VND unless the system applies a lower limit.

Cash withdrawal fee:

**4% of the withdrawn amount, minimum 100,000 VND/transaction.**

In addition to the withdrawal fee, the transaction may also start accruing interest immediately under the applicable policy.

### Advisory Principle

Cash withdrawal should only be used when necessary.

If a customer needs a large cash amount and a long repayment period, employees should explore whether another consumer loan product would be more suitable.

>
> Bank employees must not ask customers to provide OTPs, PINs, or login passwords for card application support or card processing.

Customers should be instructed to:

- Never provide an OTP to anyone.
- Never share the PIN.
- Never send full images of both sides of the card through insecure channels.
- Never save card information on public devices.
- Check the website address before entering card information.
- Be cautious with links received via text message or social media.
- Enable transaction notifications.
- Review transactions regularly.

# 18. Lost Card or Suspected Fraud

When a customer reports a lost card or detects a suspicious transaction, employees should instruct the customer to act promptly:

1. Lock the card in the application, if possible.
2. Contact the bank through an official channel.
3. Review recent transactions.
4. Identify transactions not made by the customer.
5. Provide necessary information for investigation.
6. Follow the bank's dispute or transaction investigation procedures.
7. Request card reissuance when appropriate.

Customers should not wait until the end of the statement cycle to report a suspicious transaction.

---

# 19. Supplementary Cards

Flexi Rewards supports the issuance of up to **02 supplementary cards** for one primary card account under normal policy.

The supplementary cardholder must meet the applicable eligibility conditions.

The primary cardholder may request a separate spending limit for a supplementary card, but the total spending across all cards remains within the credit limit of the primary card account.

Example:

- Account limit: 100 million VND.
- Supplementary card spending limit: 20 million VND.

This does not create an additional 20 million VND beyond the 100 million VND account limit.

Transactions made by supplementary cards are posted to the primary card account, and the primary cardholder is responsible for payment obligations in accordance with the agreement with the bank.

# 21. Handling Common Objections

## 21.1. “I am afraid credit card use will result in very high interest charges”

Credit card interest rates are higher than many secured loan products, but eligible purchase transactions do not necessarily incur interest if the customer pays the full statement balance on time.

Employees should explain at the same time:

- How the interest-free period works.
- Statement date.
- Payment due date.
- Consequences of not making full payment.

Do not simply say, “the card offers 50 days interest-free” without explaining the accompanying conditions.

---

## 21.2. “Can I just pay the minimum amount every month?”

Yes, provided the payment meets the minimum amount shown on the statement.

However, the remaining outstanding balance may incur interest.

If the customer maintains this payment pattern for a long period, the cost of using credit may increase significantly.

---

## 21.3. “What should I do if the card is lost?”

The customer should lock the card as soon as possible using the application or by contacting the bank.

Then:

- Review transactions.
- Report abnormal transactions.
- Submit a dispute/investigation request if needed.
- Request a replacement card.

---

## 21.4. “How is a credit card different from an ATM card?”

If the customer uses the term “ATM card” to refer to a debit card, explain:

A debit card uses money that the customer already has in the account.

A credit card uses a limit granted by the bank, which the customer must repay later.

---

## 21.5. “Can I withdraw cash using a credit card?”

Yes, within the cash withdrawal limit.

However, the transaction incurs a 4% fee, with a minimum of 100,000 VND, and interest may also be charged immediately under the policy.

Therefore, a credit card should not be used as a regular cash withdrawal tool.

---

## 21.6. “If I have a credit card, does that mean I have an extra 100 million VND to spend?”

It should not be understood that way.

If the card limit is 100 million VND, this is the maximum amount of credit the bank permits the customer to use within the applicable rules; it is not the customer's income.

All amounts used still create a repayment obligation.

Customers receiving income into the account may consider setting up automatic payments to reduce the risk of forgetting the due date.

## 23.2. HVA Digital Banking Application

The digital banking application helps customers:

- Monitor transactions.
- View statements.
- Check credit limits.
- Pay outstanding balances.
- Lock the card.
- Manage certain transaction functions.

Customers should use the official application provided by the bank.

## 23.3. Flexible Consumer Loan

In some cases, the customer's needs may not be suitable for credit card use.

Examples:

- A large amount of money is needed.
- The funds are needed for a long period.
- A fixed repayment schedule is preferred.
- The need is not for short-term payment.

Employees may explore the customer's needs further to determine whether a consumer loan product would be more appropriate.

Do not use a credit card as the default substitute for every borrowing need.

# 24. Notes for Sales Advisors

## 24.1. Do Not Commit to a Credit Limit

Do not use statements such as:

- “With an income of 30 million, you will definitely get a 100 million limit.”
- “This application will definitely be approved.”
- “Customers receiving salary through the bank are guaranteed to get the card.”

Preferred wording:

> “Based on the preliminary information, the application may be accepted for review. The official credit limit and approval result depend on the appraisal process.”

---

## 24.2. Do Not Focus Only on Promotions

Cashback and installment plans are supplementary benefits.

Employees must first explain:

- The nature of the credit limit.
- Interest rates.
- Fees.
- Statements.
- Due dates.
- Repayment obligations.

---

## 24.3. Clearly Explain the Interest-Free Period

Do not say:

> “This card is always interest-free for 50 days.”

Instead, clearly state:

> “The card offers a maximum interest-free period of approximately 50 days for eligible transactions when the customer satisfies the payment requirements.”

---

## 24.4. Do Not Encourage Long-Term Minimum Payments

The minimum payment is a mechanism to support the customer's obligation for the billing cycle and should not be presented as a long-term financial management method.

Customers must be warned that the remaining balance may incur interest.

---

## 24.5. Do Not Encourage Spending Close to the Credit Limit

Using nearly the entire credit limit may:

- Reduce the ability to make other transactions.
- Create repayment pressure.
- Affect the customer's financial capacity.
- Potentially affect credit assessment.

---

## 24.6. Do Not Receive OTPs, PINs, or Passwords

Employees must never ask customers to provide:

- OTP.
- PIN.
- Digital banking password.
- Email password.
- Authentication information that only the customer should know.

# 26. Principles for Responsible Card Use

When guiding customers who are using a card for the first time, employees should emphasize:

1. Spend only within the amount the customer can repay.
2. Do not treat the credit limit as income.
3. Monitor transactions regularly.
4. Review monthly statements.
5. Prioritize full and timely payment when financially able.
6. Limit cash withdrawals.
7. Do not share security information.
8. Lock the card immediately if there is any suspicion.
9. Review promotional conditions before relying on cashback benefits.
10. Proactively contact the bank when experiencing payment difficulties.
