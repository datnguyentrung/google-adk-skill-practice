# 2. Product Overview

## 2.1. Product Introduction

**Smart Cash Personal Loan** is an unsecured personal loan for eligible individual customers who need financing for lawful personal and household purposes.

The loan does not normally require collateral.

Customers may use the loan for purposes such as:

- Household expenses.
- Education-related expenses.
- Medical and healthcare expenses.
- Home furnishing and minor renovation.
- Purchase of consumer goods.
- Travel expenses.
- Wedding and family-event expenses.
- Consolidation of eligible short-term personal obligations, subject to appraisal.
- Other lawful personal consumption purposes accepted by the bank.

The product is not intended for:

- Securities speculation.
- Illegal gambling.
- Cryptocurrency speculation where prohibited by policy.
- Financing prohibited goods or services.
- Business purposes when a business-loan product is more appropriate.
- Repayment of obligations that the bank is not permitted to refinance.

---

## 2.2. Product Purpose

The product is designed to provide customers with a structured source of personal financing with:

- A defined loan amount.
- A defined repayment schedule.
- Fixed monthly installment obligations during the applicable pricing period.
- Clear interest and fee information.
- Digital or branch-based application options.
- Transparent appraisal and approval conditions.

The product should only be recommended when the customer's expected monthly repayment obligation is reasonable relative to their verified income and existing financial obligations.

# 4. Loan Amount

## 4.1. Standard Loan Amount

The standard loan amount ranges from:

**20,000,000 VND to 500,000,000 VND.**

The final approved amount depends on the customer's:

- Verified income.
- Employment stability.
- Existing debt obligations.
- Credit history.
- Internal credit score.
- Requested tenor.
- Debt service capacity.
- Other risk factors identified during appraisal.

---

## 4.2. Income-Based Guideline

Under the standard product guideline, the requested loan amount should generally not exceed:

**12 times the customer's verified average monthly net income.**

Example:

A customer has verified average monthly net income of:

**25,000,000 VND/month**

The reference maximum based only on the income multiple would be:

**300,000,000 VND**

However, this does not mean the customer will automatically be approved for 300 million VND.

The final amount may be lower after considering:

- Existing mortgage payments.
- Auto-loan payments.
- Credit-card outstanding balances.
- Other consumer loans.
- Dependents.
- Employment stability.
- Credit history.
- Internal risk limits.

---

# 5. Supported Loan Tenors

Customers may select or request one of the following standard tenors:

- 6 months.
- 12 months.
- 18 months.
- 24 months.
- 36 months.
- 48 months.

Employees should recommend a tenor by balancing:

- Monthly repayment affordability.
- Total borrowing cost.
- Customer cash-flow stability.
- Customer preference for faster debt repayment.
- Expected financial changes during the loan period.

A longer tenor may reduce the monthly payment amount but may increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

## 6.2. Interest Calculation Method

Interest is calculated on the **declining principal balance**.

A simplified monthly interest formula is:

**Monthly Interest = Outstanding Principal × Annual Interest Rate × Actual Days / 365**

The exact amount may vary based on the number of days in the billing period and system calculation rules.

Employees should not calculate a final contractual repayment amount manually when the system-generated repayment schedule is available.

# 8. Customer Eligibility

## 8.1. Mandatory Eligibility Conditions

Customers applying for Smart Cash Personal Loan must normally satisfy all of the following conditions:

1. Be an individual customer.
2. Be at least **22 years old** at the time of application.
3. Not be older than **60 years old** at the scheduled final repayment date under standard policy.
4. Hold a valid identification document.
5. Have verifiable residence information.
6. Have a lawful and verifiable source of income.
7. Have average net monthly income of at least **12,000,000 VND**.
8. Have at least **6 months of continuous employment history**.
9. Have worked for the current employer for at least **3 months**, except where policy permits another assessment approach.
10. Have an acceptable credit history.
11. Not have overdue debt classified above the bank's accepted threshold.
12. Satisfy the bank's debt-service-ratio requirements.
13. Use the loan for a lawful purpose accepted by the bank.
14. Provide complete application documents as required.

---

## 8.2. Preliminary Eligibility Does Not Mean Approval

A customer meeting the minimum age and income requirements does **not** automatically qualify for the loan.

The bank must still assess:

- Verified income.
- Employer information.
- Existing credit obligations.
- Credit-card utilization.
- Loan repayment history.
- Credit bureau records.
- Requested loan amount.
- Requested tenor.
- Debt service ratio.
- Fraud risk.
- Internal credit score.

Employees must not promise loan approval before the official appraisal result is issued.

# 9. Debt Service Ratio

## 9.1. Standard Guideline

Under the standard policy, the customer's total monthly debt obligations after the proposed loan should generally not exceed:

**50% of verified average monthly net income.**

This ratio is referred to in this document as the **Debt Service Ratio (DSR)**.

Simplified formula:

**DSR = Total Monthly Debt Obligations / Verified Average Monthly Net Income**

---

## 9.2. Example

Customer income:

**30,000,000 VND/month**

Maximum reference debt obligations at a 50% DSR:

**15,000,000 VND/month**

If the customer already pays:

- Auto loan: 6,000,000 VND/month.
- Credit-card reference obligation: 2,000,000 VND/month.

The remaining reference capacity is approximately:

**7,000,000 VND/month**

The bank may still approve a lower amount or reject the application depending on the full appraisal result.

# 17. Sales Advisory Principles

Employees must:

- Explain borrowing cost clearly.
- Avoid presenting the maximum eligible amount as the recommended amount.
- Consider the customer's repayment capacity.
- Explain early repayment fees.
- Explain late-payment consequences.
- Avoid guaranteeing approval.
- Avoid encouraging unnecessary borrowing.
- Distinguish campaign targeting from actual credit eligibility.
- Verify the final system-approved interest rate before confirmation.

Employees must not:

- Ask the customer to falsify income documents.
- Recommend hiding existing debts.
- Promise approval.
- Promise a specific risk tier before appraisal.
- Encourage the customer to borrow more than needed solely to meet a promotion threshold.

# 18. Sample Advisory Scenario

## 18.1. Customer Background

Sample customer:

- Name: Ms. Mai.
- Age: 31.
- Employment: Software project coordinator.
- Net monthly salary: approximately 32,000,000 VND.
- Salary credited to the bank for 10 months.
- Existing auto-loan payment: 5,000,000 VND/month.
- No overdue debt reported.
- Financing need: 180,000,000 VND for home furnishing.
- Preferred tenor: 24 months.

---

## 18.2. Dialogue

**Employee:** Hello Ms. Mai. I understand you are considering financing for home furnishing. Approximately how much do you expect to need?

**Customer:** Around 180 million VND.

**Employee:** Do you currently have any monthly loan or credit obligations?

**Customer:** I have an auto loan and pay around 5 million VND per month.

**Employee:** Thank you. Your monthly salary is approximately 32 million VND and is paid into your account here, correct?

**Customer:** Yes. My salary has been credited here for about ten months.

**Employee:** Based on the information you provided, Smart Cash Personal Loan may be worth considering. However, the bank still needs to perform a full credit appraisal before confirming the approved amount, tenor, and interest rate.

**Customer:** What interest rate would I get?

**Employee:** The standard rates currently depend on the approved risk tier. Tier A is 15.0% per year, Tier B is 17.5%, and Tier C is 20.0%. During the Smart Salary Loan 2026 promotion, eligible salaried customers may receive a reduction of 1.5 percentage points per year.

**Customer:** Would I qualify for that promotion?

**Employee:** You appear to meet some preliminary conditions because your salary is credited to the bank and the amount you want to borrow is above 100 million VND. However, promotional eligibility is only confirmed after the underlying loan is approved and all program conditions are checked.

**Customer:** Can I borrow the full 180 million VND?

**Employee:** You may request 180 million VND, but I cannot guarantee the final amount. The bank will consider your verified income, your 5 million VND monthly auto-loan obligation, credit history, requested tenor, and other risk factors.

**Customer:** What happens if I want to pay everything off early?

**Employee:** Early repayment is allowed. Under the current standard fee schedule, the fee is 3% of the prepaid principal during the first 12 months, 2% during months 13 to 24, and 1% after month 24.

**Customer:** I think 24 months is reasonable.

**Employee:** That can be assessed. A 24-month tenor may reduce the monthly payment compared with a shorter tenor, but the total interest paid over the loan period may be higher. Once your application is approved, the system will provide the exact repayment schedule for you to review before accepting the contract.

# 19. Handling Common Objections

## 19.1. “Why can't you tell me the exact interest rate now?”

The final interest rate depends on the customer's approved risk tier and applicable promotional conditions.

Employees may provide the current pricing range but must not commit to a final rate before appraisal.

---

## 19.2. “My income is above the minimum, so the loan will definitely be approved, right?”

No.

Minimum income is only one eligibility condition.

The bank also assesses:

- Existing debt.
- Debt service ratio.
- Credit history.
- Employment stability.
- Requested amount.
- Fraud risk.
- Internal credit score.

---

## 19.3. “Can I borrow the maximum 500 million VND?”

The product supports loan amounts up to 500 million VND, but the maximum product limit is not an automatic customer entitlement.

The approved amount depends on the customer's financial capacity and appraisal result.

---

## 19.4. “Should I borrow 100 million VND just to qualify for the promotion?”

Employees should not encourage a customer to borrow more than they genuinely need solely to satisfy a promotion threshold.

The customer's financing need and repayment capacity must remain the primary considerations.

---

## 19.5. “Can I repay the loan early?”

Yes.

Early repayment is supported, but an early repayment fee may apply based on when the repayment occurs.
